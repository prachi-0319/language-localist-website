The Post-DTP Linguistic QA Process

A beautiful layout means nothing if the text is compromised. Every finalized document undergoes a rigorous Quality Assurance (QA) review by a native linguist to verify that no text was truncated, fonts render correctly, and cultural aesthetics are preserved.

dashboard_customize
Step 01

DTP Formatting

Translated text is flowed into the source layout. Engineers handle text expansion, font substitution, RTL mirroring, and script-specific spacing.

record_voice_over
Step 02

Linguistic Review

A native linguist reviews the formatted document end-to-end, checking for truncation, rendering errors, missing glyphs, and cultural appropriateness.

task_alt
Step 03

Final Approval

The approved document is exported in your required format (PDF, IDML, AI, etc.) and delivered with a quality certificate.
